Margin vs. Markup: What is the Difference?

While both metrics measure business profitability, they use different baselines:

  • Profit Margin: The percentage of the selling price that is profit. Formula: (Profit / Price) × 100
  • Markup: The percentage added to the cost price to reach the selling price. Formula: (Profit / Cost) × 100

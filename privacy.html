Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

At BizMathTools, accessible from bizmathtools.com, your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by BizMathTools and how we use it.

2. Calculation Data

All calculations performed on our tools (such as CBM, freelance rates, and proforma invoices) are processed locally within your web browser. We do not store, save, or transmit any calculation inputs or sensitive financial data to our servers.

3. Google Analytics and Cookies

We use Google Analytics to analyze site traffic, user behavior, and geographic distribution to improve our tools. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect standard internet log information in an anonymous form. You can opt-out of Google Analytics by adjusting your browser settings.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding our privacy policy, feel free to reach out via our contact page.
